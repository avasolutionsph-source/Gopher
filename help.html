Getting started

What is Gopher, and who can use it?

Gopher is a student-to-student platform with three services:

  • Errands — book a fellow student (a go-runner) to print, buy, fetch, or deliver things for you.
  • Rentals — borrow gear like umbrellas, calculators, and cameras from students nearby.
  • Notes & Books — reviewers, lecture notes, and study guides made by students, plus books to rent or buy.

Anyone can both ask for help and earn by helping. Gopher is for currently enrolled college students, verified with a student ID or matriculation form. The pilot is at Ateneo de Naga University (ADNU); the University of Nueva Caceres (UNC), Universidad de Sta. Isabel (USI), and Naga College Foundation (NCF) are next. You must be 18 or older to be a go-runner or a lender.

Gopher is a class project prototype. The demo doesn’t take real payments.

How do I verify that I’m a student?

When you sign up, upload a clear photo of your current student ID, or of your matriculation form for this semester. Our team checks that the name matches your account and that you’re currently enrolled, usually within a few minutes during pilot hours. Then your profile gets a verified badge.

You can browse right away, but only verified students can book, list, or earn. We use the photo only for this check and delete it once we’re done. If it’s blurry or the name doesn’t match, we’ll ask you to upload it again. Still stuck? Contact support.

In the demo, the check is simulated and your photo never leaves your device.

Errands

What can a go-runner do for me, and what does it cost?

Go-runners can print & photocopy, buy food & drinks, get an item from another location, deliver or pick up items, buy school supplies, and buy medicine & personal necessities (over-the-counter only).

Pilot pricing (final pricing is still being set):

  • Errand fee, paid to your go-runner: printing ₱20; food & drinks, getting an item, delivery or pickup, and school supplies ₱25; medicine & personal necessities ₱30. You can always offer more.
  • Rush (within 30 minutes): +₱10.
  • Printing cost: ₱2 per page black & white, ₱5 per page color, at cost.
  • Items: no markup. You pay the receipt price.
  • Service fee: ₱5 per booking.

Example: 10 pages × 2 copies in black & white is ₱40 printing + ₱20 errand fee + ₱5 service fee = ₱65.

Which errands aren’t allowed?

Go-runners can’t buy or deliver:

  • alcohol, tobacco, or vapes
  • prescription-only medicine
  • anything illegal or dangerous

No academic dishonesty either: go-runners won’t take exams or quizzes, do graded work, or sign attendance for anyone.

During the pilot, errands run from 7 AM to 7 PM, and items can cost up to ₱500 per errand. Go-runners never enter private rooms or dorms — hand-offs happen at public spots. The full list is in our Terms.

How do I cancel an errand, and what does it cost?
  • Before a go-runner accepts: free.
  • After a go-runner accepts: ₱10 goes to the go-runner for their time, and the rest is refunded.
  • After the go-runner has bought or printed your items: you can’t cancel anymore. If something’s wrong, report a problem on the booking instead.

GCash refunds come out of Gopher Hold and go back to the account you paid with.

Rentals

How does renting work, and can I cancel?

Pick an item and your dates, pay (plus any refundable deposit), and meet the lender at a public spot on campus. At pickup, you show your hand-off code and the lender enters it. At the return it’s the other way around: the lender shows their code and you enter it.

Cancelling is free any time before pickup. If the lender declines your request, you get a full refund.

Pilot prices per day: umbrella, raincoat, or powerbank ₱15; scientific calculator ₱20; tripod ₱50; ring light ₱60; lapel mic ₱80; camera ₱300. Books are ₱50 per week.

How do deposits work?

Some items need a refundable deposit: tripod ₱200, ring light ₱250, lapel mic ₱300, camera ₱1,000. Deposits are paid by GCash and stay in Gopher Hold during the rental.

After you return the item and the lender checks it, your deposit comes back to you, minus any late fees or damage charges.

Example: a tripod for 2 days is ₱100 + ₱5 service fee + ₱200 deposit = ₱305 now, and ₱200 back after you return it.

What if I return something late, or it gets damaged or lost?

Late: you get a 30-minute grace period. After that, one day’s rate comes out of your deposit for each late day. Running late? Message the lender in the app — they may agree to extend.

Damaged or lost: tell the lender and report it in the app right away. Gopher support looks at both sides and decides what’s fair. Costs are charged to the deposit first; if they’re more than the deposit, support works out the rest with both of you.

Tip: check the item at pickup and send photos of any existing scratches in the chat.

Notes & books

What notes and reviewers can I sell?

Only material you made yourself: your own reviewers, lecture notes, and study guides. You can also rent out or sell physical books you own.

Not allowed:

  • photocopied or scanned textbooks, or anyone else’s work
  • exam answer keys, leaked exams, or graded work (yours or anyone’s)

If someone reports a listing, it’s hidden while we review it. If the report stands, the listing is removed, buyers are refunded, and the seller gets a strike. See Academic materials & intellectual property.

Can I cancel or get a refund on a reviewer?

You can cancel any purchase for free until the seller confirms it. Digital purchases are final after that — but if a reviewer isn’t as described, or it looks copied, report it within 24 hours and we’ll look into it.

Pilot prices: buy a digital copy for ₱30–80, or rent one for 7 days for ₱15–25, plus the ₱5 service fee.

Payments

What is Gopher Hold?

When you pay by GCash, the money doesn’t go straight to the other student. Gopher holds it until the hand-off is confirmed with the hand-off code, then releases it to the go-runner, lender, or seller.

If the booking is cancelled, the money comes back to you under the cancellation rules. Rental deposits stay in Gopher Hold until the lender checks the return. Cash payments aren’t covered by Gopher Hold.

What is the service fee?

₱5 per booking. It’s how Gopher earns, and you always see it before you pay. Go-runners and lenders keep 100% of their fee, and items are passed through at cost with no markup.

Can I pay with cash?

Yes — for errands and for rentals without a deposit, up to ₱200 per booking. Pay at the hand-off, after you’ve checked what you got.

Deposits and digital items (like reviewers) need GCash. Cash isn’t covered by Gopher Hold, so Gopher can’t hold or refund it. For bigger bookings, GCash is safer.

Safety

What’s a hand-off code?

A 4-digit code that proves the right person got the item. The person receiving shows their code, and the person handing over enters it in the app.

For an errand, you show your code and your go-runner enters it. For a rental, the renter shows the code at pickup, and the lender shows theirs at the return. Don’t share your code until you have the item in hand.

How do I stay safe, and how do I report or block someone?
  • Meet at public spots on campus. Go-runners never enter private rooms or dorms.
  • Keep chats in the app. You never need to share your phone number.
  • Check ratings. Only students who completed a booking with someone can rate them.

To report or block someone, open their profile or the booking and choose Report or Block. Support replies within 24 hours. If you’re ever in danger, get somewhere safe and contact campus security or call 911 first.

Earning

How do go-runners get paid?

When the requester shows their hand-off code and you enter it, Gopher Hold releases the money to your wallet in the app: your full errand fee, plus the receipt amount for anything you bought. From your wallet, you can move the balance to your GCash.

For cash bookings, the requester pays you in person at the hand-off. Either way, you keep 100% of your fee — the ₱5 service fee is paid by the requester. Keep your receipts; requesters pay the receipt price.

Who can earn, and how do strikes work?

Any verified student who is 18 or older can be a go-runner or a lender. Anyone verified can sell reviewers and notes they wrote themselves.

You get a strike for cancelling after you’ve accepted a booking, for not showing up, or when a report against you is upheld. Three strikes in 30 days pauses your account. Serious problems, like harassment or fraud, can lead to suspension right away.

Account & privacy

How do I download or delete my data?

In the app, go to Profile → Download my data to get a copy of your profile, bookings, and ratings. To close your account, go to Profile → Delete account. Some booking records are kept for as long as the law requires, like payment records.

In this demo, everything you enter stays in your browser’s local storage on this device. Tapping Reset in the demo, or clearing your browser’s site data, erases it. More in our Privacy Policy.

Who can see my information?

Other students see your first name and last initial, your rating, and the meetup spot for a booking. Your phone number is never shown. Print files only go to the go-runner assigned to your errand, and they’re deleted 24 hours after delivery. We never sell your data.
