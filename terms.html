The short version

  • Gopher connects students who need a hand with students who can help. The students doing the work aren’t Gopher staff.
  • Pay by GCash and Gopher Hold keeps your money until the hand-off code is entered.
  • Meet at public spots on campus, keep chats in the app, and keep academic work your own.
  • Breaking the rules earns strikes. Three strikes in 30 days pauses your account.

1. Who can use Gopher

  • You must be a currently enrolled college student and verify your account with your current student ID or matriculation form. The name on it must match your account.
  • You must be 18 or older to work as a go-runner or to list items as a lender.
  • One account per person. Keep your login to yourself; you’re responsible for what happens on your account.
  • During the pilot, Gopher is open to students of Ateneo de Naga University. We plan to open at the University of Nueva Caceres, Universidad de Sta. Isabel, and Naga College Foundation next.
  • If you’re no longer enrolled, we may ask you to verify again or close your account.

2. What Gopher is

Gopher is a platform that connects independent students: requesters and go-runners for errands, renters and lenders for rentals, and buyers and sellers for notes and books.

Go-runners, lenders, and sellers are not Gopher employees, agents, or staff. They’re fellow students using the platform. Gopher doesn’t run errands, own rental items, or write the notes that are listed. We provide the app, payments through Gopher Hold, safety tools, and support.

Gopher is run by Gopher Solutions Co., a partnership established in 2026 in Naga City. It isn’t an official service of any school named on this site.

3. Bookings

  • A booking is an agreement between two students about what, where, when, and how much. You always see the full price, including the ₱5 service fee, before you confirm.
  • An errand is confirmed when a go-runner accepts it, a rental when the lender accepts it, and a purchase when the seller confirms it.
  • Describe errands, items, and notes honestly, including an item’s condition and any defects.
  • Keep details and changes in the in-app chat, so support can help if something goes wrong.
  • A hand-off is complete when the hand-off code is entered: the person receiving shows a 4-digit code, and the person handing over enters it.

4. Payments, service fee and Gopher Hold

  • GCash first. You’ll see the payment options at checkout. Cash has its own rules (see Cash on hand-off).
  • Service fee. Gopher charges ₱5 per booking. Go-runners and lenders keep 100% of their fee.
  • No markup. Errand items are charged at the receipt price. Printing is passed through at cost: ₱2 per page black & white, ₱5 per page color.
  • Gopher Hold. When you pay by GCash, Gopher holds the payment until the hand-off is confirmed, then releases it to the go-runner, lender, or seller. If a booking is cancelled, the held amount is refunded under the cancellation rules.
  • Report before you confirm. If something is wrong, report it before entering or showing the hand-off code. Money stays in Gopher Hold while support reviews a report.
  • Pilot pricing. Current prices are pilot prices and may change; final pricing is still being set. See the current pilot prices.
  • Gopher will never ask for your GCash MPIN or password. Don’t share them with anyone.

5. Cash on hand-off

  • Cash is allowed for errands and for rentals without a deposit, up to ₱200 per booking.
  • Deposits and digital items (like reviewers) must be paid by GCash.
  • Pay cash only at the hand-off, after checking what you received, and still use the hand-off code.
  • Cash payments aren’t covered by Gopher Hold. Gopher can’t hold or refund cash, but you can still report a problem.

7. Rentals

Pickup

Meet at the agreed public spot on campus. Check the item before you take it, and send photos of any existing damage in the chat. The renter shows the hand-off code and the lender enters it.

Returns

Return the item on time, in the same condition, at the agreed spot. The lender shows their code and the renter enters it, then the lender checks the item and confirms the return.

Deposits

Some items need a refundable deposit — tripod ₱200, ring light ₱250, lapel mic ₱300, camera ₱1,000. Deposits are paid by GCash and kept in Gopher Hold, and they’re refunded after the lender checks the return.

Late returns

There’s a 30-minute grace period. After that, one day’s rate per late day comes out of the deposit.

Damage and lost items

Report damage or loss right away. Support reviews both sides and decides what’s fair. Costs are charged to the deposit first, up to its full amount; if they’re more than the deposit, support will help settle the rest.

Lenders

Only list items you own. Describe them honestly, and hand them over clean and working.

8. Errands that aren’t allowed

Go-runners can’t be asked to buy, carry, or deliver:

  • alcohol
  • tobacco and vapes
  • prescription-only medicine (over-the-counter medicine is fine)
  • illegal items
  • anything dangerous, like weapons, fireworks, or flammable chemicals

No academic dishonesty. Go-runners won’t:

  • take exams or quizzes for anyone
  • do graded work, like assignments, papers, projects, or lab reports
  • sign attendance for someone

During the pilot:

  • The item budget is up to ₱500 per errand.
  • Errands run from 7 AM to 7 PM.
  • Go-runners never enter private rooms or dorms. Hand-offs happen at public spots.

A go-runner can decline any errand that feels unsafe or breaks these rules, without getting a strike.

9. Academic materials & intellectual property

  • Only list notes, reviewers, and study guides you made yourself.
  • Physical books must be copies you own.
  • Not allowed: photocopied or scanned textbooks or chapters, other people’s notes, exam answer keys or leaked exams, and graded work (yours or anyone else’s).
  • By listing, you confirm the material is your own work and that sharing it doesn’t break your school’s rules or the Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 8293).
  • You keep the rights to your work. You let Gopher show your listing and deliver copies to buyers. Buyers may use materials for their own studying only, and may not resell or share them.

When a listing is reported: it’s hidden while we review it. If the report stands, the listing is removed, buyers are refunded, and the seller gets a strike. Repeated or serious violations can lead to suspension.

10. Ratings & reviews

  • Ratings and reviews can only come from completed bookings, and both students can rate each other.
  • Keep reviews honest and about the booking. No insults, threats, or personal information.
  • Don’t pay for, trade, or pressure anyone for a rating.
  • We may remove reviews that break these rules.

11. Safety & conduct

  • Meet at public campus meetup spots. Go-runners never enter private rooms or dorms.
  • Use the in-app chat. You never need to share your phone number, so please don’t ask for anyone else’s.
  • Be respectful. No harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted contact outside the app.
  • You can report or block anyone from their profile or the booking. Support replies within 24 hours.
  • In an emergency, contact campus security or call 911 first, then tell us.

12. Strikes & suspension

You can get a strike for:

  • cancelling after you’ve accepted a booking
  • not showing up to a hand-off
  • a report against you that support upholds

Three strikes within 30 days pauses your account. Each strike counts for 30 days. Serious violations — such as harassment, fraud, theft, or listing copied material again after a warning — can lead to suspension right away. You can appeal a strike or suspension by contacting support.

13. Liability

  • Go-runners, lenders, and sellers are responsible for the errands, items, and materials they provide.
  • We work to keep Gopher safe and running, but we can’t promise it will always be available or free of errors.
  • As far as the law allows, Gopher isn’t responsible for indirect losses, and our responsibility for a booking is limited to what you paid for it through Gopher.
  • Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under Philippine law, including the Consumer Act of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 7394).

14. Disputes & support

  • If something goes wrong, report a problem on the booking or contact support. We reply within 24 hours, in English, Filipino, or Bikol.
  • While we review a report, money in Gopher Hold stays on hold.
  • Support looks at the chat, photos, receipts, and hand-off records, then decides how to resolve it — for example, a refund, a partial refund, or releasing the payment.
  • If you disagree with a decision, reply to your ticket and a different team member will take a second look.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the Philippines.

15. Changes

We may update these terms as Gopher grows. If we make important changes, we’ll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
