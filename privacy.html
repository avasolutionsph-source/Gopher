The short version

  • We collect what we need to run bookings safely: your name, email, a photo of your student ID or matriculation form (only to confirm you’re enrolled), your profile, bookings, chats, and ratings.
  • Other students see only your first name and last initial, your rating, and the meetup spot.
  • We never sell your data, and we never ask for your GCash MPIN or password.
  • You can download or delete your data from your Profile.
  • This demo keeps everything in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

1. Who we are

Gopher is run by Gopher Solutions Co., a partnership established in 2026 in Naga City, Camarines Sur, Philippines. For the personal information described here, Gopher Solutions Co. is the personal information controller.

Data Protection Officer: dpo@gopher.example (placeholder). Write to our Data Protection Officer about anything in this policy, or to use your rights.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email, school, program, and year level, plus your password (stored in protected, hashed form).
  • Student verification: a photo of your current student ID or matriculation form. We use it only to confirm you’re currently enrolled. After the check we keep the result (verified, which document, and the date), not the photo.
  • Profile details: an optional photo and short bio, your usual meetup spots, and confirmation that you’re 18 or older if you become a go-runner or lender.
  • Bookings: what you requested or listed, meetup spots, times, prices, receipts, and hand-off records.
  • Chats: the messages you send in the app.
  • Ratings & reviews: the ones you give and receive.
  • Payments: GCash payment references, amounts, and refund records.
  • Print files: the files you upload for printing errands.
  • Support: the messages and reports you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device & usage: app version, device type, and error logs, so we can keep Gopher working and secure.

We never collect your GCash MPIN or password. Gopher will never ask for them — not in the app, not in chat, not by email.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and confirm you’re a currently enrolled student, using the student ID or matriculation form you upload.
  • To run bookings: matching requesters with go-runners, renters with lenders, and buyers with sellers; handling Gopher Hold payments, refunds, and hand-off codes.
  • To keep everyone safe: ratings, reports, strikes, and fraud prevention.
  • To give support and resolve disputes.
  • To improve Gopher, using summarized data where we can.
  • To send service messages like booking updates and receipts. We only send marketing messages if you opt in.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping payment records.

4. Legal basis

Under RA 10173, we process your personal information on these grounds:

  • Consent — for example, optional profile details and marketing messages. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract — to provide the bookings and services you ask for.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records the law requires and to respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep Gopher safe, prevent fraud, and improve the service, in ways that don’t override your rights.

We don’t ask for sensitive personal information, like health details or government ID numbers. If an errand involves medicine or personal necessities, share only what your go-runner needs to buy it.

6. Storage, security & retention

  • We protect your data with safeguards like encryption in transit, access controls, and limited staff access.
  • Student ID and matriculation photos are deleted as soon as we finish checking them (within 7 days at most).
  • Print files are deleted 24 hours after delivery.
  • Booking and payment records are kept for as long as the law requires.
  • Chats and reports are kept only as long as needed for support and safety.
  • When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your profile, except for records we must keep by law.
  • If a personal data breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

7. Your rights as a data subject

Under RA 10173, you have the right:

  • to be informed about how your personal data is collected and used;
  • to access your personal data;
  • to object to processing, including for marketing;
  • to erasure or blocking of data that’s incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or no longer needed;
  • to rectification, so mistakes get corrected;
  • to data portability, meaning a copy of your data in a format you can reuse;
  • to damages if you’re harmed by inaccurate, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of your data; and
  • to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

How to use your rights

  • In the app, go to Profile → Download my data to get a copy of your data, or Profile → Delete account to erase it.
  • Edit your profile any time to correct your details.
  • For anything else, email our Data Protection Officer at dpo@gopher.example.
  • You can also reach the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

8. Cookies & local storage

  • Gopher uses your browser’s local storage (and, in a real launch, essential cookies) to keep you signed in and remember settings.
  • We don’t use advertising cookies, and this website doesn’t use analytics or tracking scripts.

9. About this demo

This site is a class project prototype. Here’s what happens to what you type:

  • All data you enter stays in your browser’s local storage on this device.
  • Nothing is sent to a server. There are no real payments, emails, or texts.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data, or tapping Reset in the demo, removes it.
  • The forms on this website, like “Notify me” and the support form, are demos too. Nothing you type in them is sent.

Please don’t enter real personal details. Use made-up names and information when you try the demo.
