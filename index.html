How it works

Help in three taps.

Need a hand, or have a free period? Either way, it works like this.

Get help

  1. 1

    Post it

    Say what you need, where you are, and by when. You see the full price before you book.

  2. 2

    Pay with Gopher Hold

    Pay by GCash. Gopher holds the money until the hand-off, and refunds it if the booking is cancelled.

  3. 3

    Show your hand-off code

    When your go-runner arrives, show your 4-digit code. They enter it, and you’re done.

Earn

  1. 1

    Go online or list an item

    Free period? Go online as a go-runner, or list gear and reviewers you made yourself.

  2. 2

    Do the job or hand it over

    Accept errands that fit your schedule, or meet a renter at a public spot on campus.

  3. 3

    Get paid to your wallet

    Enter the other student’s hand-off code. Gopher Hold releases your pay right away.

Safety

Student to student, safe by design.

Every booking is between verified students, and your money stays protected until the hand-off.

  • Verified students

    Everyone uploads a student ID or matriculation form, and our team checks it before they can book or earn.

  • Gopher Hold

    GCash payments wait with Gopher until the hand-off is confirmed. Cancelled? The money goes back to you.

  • Hand-off codes

    The person receiving shows a 4-digit code, and the person handing over enters it. It proves the right person got it.

  • Ratings & reviews from real bookings

    Only students who finished a booking with you can rate you — and you rate them too.

  • Public meetup spots & in-app chat

    Meet at public spots on campus and talk in the app. No phone numbers shared.

  • Support replies within 24 hours

    Report a problem anytime. We reply within 24 hours, in English, Filipino, or Bikol.

Visit the Help center
Is Gopher a real service yet?

Not yet. Gopher is a class project by BSBA Marketing Management students at Ateneo de Naga University. The demo shows how it would work: no real payments, no real go-runners, and nothing you type leaves your browser.

Who can use Gopher?

Currently enrolled college students who verify with their student ID or matriculation form. The pilot starts at Ateneo de Naga University, with UNC, USI and NCF next. You need to be 18 or older to be a go-runner or to lend items.

How much does it cost?

Errand fees start at ₱20 and go to your go-runner. Items cost what the receipt says, with no markup, and printing is ₱2 a page (₱5 for color). Gopher adds a ₱5 service fee per booking. Rentals start at ₱15 a day.

These are pilot prices. Final pricing is still being set.

What happens to my money after I pay?

When you pay by GCash, Gopher Hold keeps the money until the hand-off is confirmed with your 4-digit hand-off code. Then it’s released to your go-runner, lender, or seller. If the booking is cancelled, you get your money back under the cancellation rules.

Can I pay with cash?

Yes, for errands and no-deposit rentals up to ₱200 — you pay at the hand-off. Deposits and digital items need GCash. Cash payments aren’t covered by Gopher Hold.

How do I start earning?

Verify with your student ID or matriculation form, then go online as a go-runner during a free period, or list gear and reviewers you made yourself. You keep 100% of your fee. Go-runners and lenders must be 18 or older.
